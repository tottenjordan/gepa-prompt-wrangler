GEPA Prompt Wrangler — Logo/Graphic Concept

A cosmic cowboy figure (space wrangler) riding through a stylized neural network
constellation, lassoing floating prompt bubbles and AI model icons. The scene
blends Southern rock psychedelia with modern AI/tech aesthetics.

Visual elements:
- A figure in a cowboy hat and space suit, riding a beam of light through a
  star-filled sky (homage to Widespread Panic's "Space Wrangler")
- The lasso is made of glowing data streams, wrapping around floating text
  bubbles labeled with prompt snippets
- Multiple AI model icons float like planets in orbit: a Gemini twin-star icon
  and a Claude/Anthropic shield, representing the multi-model aspect
- Below the figure, a landscape of circuit-board terrain with MCP tool servers
  as small outposts/buildings
- A pipeline flows left to right across the bottom: Deploy → Eval → Optimize →
  Redeploy → Eval → Report, each step as a glowing waypoint
- Color palette: deep space purples and blues with warm orange/gold accents
  (like stage lights), neon green data streams
- Title "GEPA PROMPT WRANGLER" in a weathered serif font with a subtle glow,
  positioned at the top
- Subtitle: "Wrangle your prompts across the model universe" in smaller text
- Stars and nebula clouds in the background suggest the vast space of possible
  prompts being explored by the evolutionary optimization algorithm
