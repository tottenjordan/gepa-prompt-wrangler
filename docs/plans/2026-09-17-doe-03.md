## The design

### Collect one pool, resample every cell from it

**6 captures × 5 scorings each = 30 scoring passes**, from one engine. Every
(`num_runs` r, `score_repeats` s) cell is then reconstructed **offline** by resampling that
grid, so the campaign pays once for data that answers the whole surface.

A control-arm delta at (r, s) is built exactly the way production builds an eval:

```
pick two DISJOINT sets of r captures      -> "before" and "after"
per capture: combine_results(s of its 5 scorings)   # the score_repeats step
per side:    combine_results(the r capture results) # the num_runs step
delta = after - before, per metric
```

**This mirrors `run_batch_eval_averaged()` exactly** — it is `num_runs` × [one inference +
`score_repeats` scorings] (`evaluator.py:1450`). Task 0 makes that literally true.

Disjoint splits available: **r=1 → 15**, **r=2 → 45**, **r=3 → 10**.

### The headline is iso-cost contrasts, not the full grid

At ~2.6 min/capture and ~2.8 min/scoring pass (DOE 02 measured), cost(r,s) = r·2.6 + r·s·2.8.
Three cells cost the same ~16.5 min, and two cost ~11 — **that is the actual decision a
campaign designer makes**:

| budget | option A | option B | option C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ~11 min | **(r=2, s=1)** 10.8 | **(r=1, s=3)** 11.0 | — |
| ~16.5 min | **(r=3, s=1)** 16.2 | **(r=2, s=2)** 16.4 | **(r=1, s=5)** 16.6 |

`(3,1)` is today's default. `(1,5)` costs the same and never touches the engine.

### Pre-registered directional predictions — per metric, from DOE 02

| metric | judge sd (DOE 02 arm 1) | disagreement | prediction at ~16.5 min |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `instruction_following_v1` | 0.024 | **64/64** | **(1,5) beats (3,1)** — judge-dominated |
| `safety_v1` | **0.000** | **0/64** | **(3,1) beats (1,5)**; `score_repeats` does ~nothing |
| `final_response_quality_v1` | 0.023 | 59.4% | (1,5) ≥ (3,1), weaker |
| `hallucination_v1` | 0.010 | 43.8% | no strong call |
| `tool_use_quality_v1` | 0.010 | 21.9% | no strong call |

Two metrics make opposite, falsifiable predictions from the same data. **If `safety_v1`'s
floor falls with `score_repeats`, DOE 02's judge/agent split is wrong** and the guidance now
in CLAUDE.md has to be withdrawn.

### Pre-registration

- **Report a quantile, not only the max.** `measure_noise_floor_per_metric` uses
  `max(|delta|)` over a handful of control arms. **A max over 10 bootstrap draws and a max
  over 1,000 are not the same statistic** — the bootstrap max grows with draw count and
  would manufacture a scary-looking floor. Report **median, 95th percentile, and max**, and
  when comparing to CLAUDE.md's 0.058 / 0.011–0.014, compare **max-over-a-matched-number-of-draws**.
- Report **per-metric**, never one pooled number. The 2026-09-02 control arm spanned 0.0028
  to 0.0747 — a 27× range.
- Report **paired and unpaired** at every cell, so pairing's value is visible rather than
  asserted at "~15%".
- **Cells are not independent.** Every cell is resampled from the same 6 captures, so these
  are sampling distributions *conditional on this pool*. Say it next to every table.
- **No cell is dropped for looking noisy.** A noisy cell is the finding.
- **Do not re-derive the judge/agent split from this pool.** DOE 02 already showed n=5 sds
  have a 95% interval of [0.007, 0.033] around a true 0.020.

## Task 0: Make `num_runs` and `score_repeats` share their arithmetic

**Files:** `wrangler/eval/evaluator.py` (`run_batch_eval_averaged`), `tests/test_score_repeats.py`

`combine_results()`'s docstring claims it is *"shared by the two knobs so they cannot drift
apart"*. **It is not.** It has exactly one call site (`evaluator.py:1024`, the
`score_repeats` path); `run_batch_eval_averaged` re-implements the same mean/stdev/
`average_per_case`/token/coverage logic inline at `:1505–1545`.

The arithmetic is currently equivalent, so this is not a bug report — but the resampling in
Task 2 claims to reproduce production, and that claim should rest on calling the same
function rather than on two implementations happening to agree.

1. **Failing test first:** assert `run_batch_eval_averaged`'s outer averaging goes through
   `combine_results` (spy or monkeypatch), and that a run returning no scores is skipped
   rather than counted as zero.
2. Replace the inline block with `combine_results(all_results, label="runs")`. Keep the
   existing progress prints and the coverage warning.
3. **One deliberate difference to preserve:** the current code returns `num_runs=num_runs`
   (requested) while `combine_results` returns `len(scored)` (successful). Pick successful,
   and assert it — a run that silently dropped 2 of 3 passes should not report 3.

**ETA: 30–45 min.**

## Task 1: Collect the pool

**Files:** `scripts/doe03_collect.py` (new)

**Step 1 — health pre-flight, and it gates.** `run_probe()` the engine before spending
anything. A capture from a degraded engine loses cases and contaminates every cell built
from it. Abort below the gate rather than proceeding — record the reach in the output.

**Step 2 — interleave the captures across the whole run, do not take them back-to-back.**
Production `eval_before` and `eval_after` are *hours* apart; six captures taken in the first
15 minutes would be more correlated than the thing being modelled and would **understate the
floor**. Order the work `capture₁, score₁×5, capture₂, score₂×5, …` so the captures span the
full ~2 h. This is free and is the single largest external-validity lever in the design.

**Step 3 — resume, and guard credentials.** ~2 h unattended. Reuse
`scripts/run_campaign.py`'s `ensure_credentials()` — a stale ADC killed both the campaign 08
driver and the m01 watcher mid-run. Skip work already on disk so a restart costs nothing.

**Step 4 — persist per pass with a parseable label**, e.g. `doe03-c{i}-pass{j}`. The CLI
path already saves every pass with per-case rows (`cli.py:474`); call the library directly
and match that shape.

**Step 5 — verify the premise before trusting anything.** Hash each capture before and after
its 5 scorings and assert scoring does not mutate it. DOE 02 did this; if responses can
drift, the judge-only cells measure nothing.

**ETA: 45–60 min to write. ~2–2.5 h unattended compute** (6×2.6 + 30×2.8 ≈ 100 min plus
overhead; DOE 02's measured overhead ran ~40% above the per-unit sum).

## Task 2: Resample and analyse

**Files:** `scripts/analyze_doe03.py` (new)

**Step 1.** Load the 6×5 grid from disk. Fail loudly on a missing cell — a silently
short grid biases every quantile.

**Step 2.** Build cells with `combine_results` (never a local mean), enumerating disjoint
before/after splits. Cap draws per cell and **report the draw count next to every number**,
because the max is draw-count dependent.

**Step 3.** For each metric × cell emit: median |Δ|, p95 |Δ|, max |Δ|, paired and unpaired,
and the split count.

**Step 4 — the iso-cost verdict table**, the campaign's actual output: at ~11 min and
~16.5 min, which option is tightest per metric, and does it match the pre-registered
prediction. Mark agreements and disagreements explicitly.

**Step 5 — answer old DOE 03 for free:** the corrected floor at (3,1) versus CLAUDE.md's
0.011–0.014, and whether the floor falls as √r — fit the exponent rather than assuming it,
and hand the result to Task 3 as `scaling_exponent`.

**Step 6 — a guard, in the spirit of `analyze_toolset_loss.py`.** If any cell has fewer
splits than a floor (say 8), print that cell as `UNDERPOWERED` rather than a number.

**ETA: 1.5–2 h.**

## Risks

- **Back-to-back captures understate the floor.** Mitigated by interleaving (Task 1 step 2);
  state the residual in the write-up, since even 2 h is shorter than a real campaign's gap.
- **Resampled cells share data.** Not independent; conditional on one pool of 6. Report it
  beside every table rather than once in a preamble.
- **r=3 has only 10 disjoint splits.** The most decision-relevant cell is the weakest
  measured. Print the split count next to it and do not quote a max from it as if it were
  the 45-split r=2 number.
- **One engine, one draw.** This is *within-engine* noise and excludes the deployment lottery
  DOE 01 measured at 0–100% reach. Correct scope for this question, wrong scope for "how much
  does an arm vary" — say so in the write-up rather than letting the number travel.
- **The engines age out of the traffic window ~2026-10-11.** After that this campaign
  inherits a health-gated deploy. Run before then.
- **`tool_use_quality_v1` crossed a re-baseline on 2026-09-17.** All scorings here use the
  hardened prompt, so the campaign is internally consistent; do not compare its per-case
  numbers to anything from before that date.
